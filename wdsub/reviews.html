My Song Reviews

Here are songs that i like by music and by song meanings

Animals - Architects

Animals is about the exploration of humanity's inability to change it's ways, the destruction of the planet, and a call to take personal responsibility than to point fingers at others. I like this for the message and the overall feel of the song.

Blackhole - Architects

Blackhole is a song about despondancy, morality, and a search for meaning in an indifferent, fleeting world. Basically, it's like a "blackhole" is consuming you and you can't escape. Feeling helpless and crushed and having an emotional collapse.
