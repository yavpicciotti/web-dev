How To Make Chocolate Chip Cookies

  1. Ingredients

    1. 1/3 cup of sugar
    2. 1/3 cup of flour
    3. 1/4 cup of baking soda
    4. cookie dough

  2. Prep Work

    1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees
    2. have two trays out
    3. Have your ingredients ready
    4. Mix ingredients and form a cookie dough circles

  3. Chocolate Chips

    1. Grab a box of chocolate chips
    2. Put on top of cookie dough
    3. place in oven for 400 degrees for 18 minutes
    4. When done get ready for cooling

  4. Cooling

    1. Always place on a cooling tray or rack
    2. Let cool for 5 minutes
    3. if ya want to make another batch,the same for those
    4. Get ready to eat and enjoy

  5. EAT
